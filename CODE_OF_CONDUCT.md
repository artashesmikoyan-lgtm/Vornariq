# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in the Vornariq community a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity,
sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste,
color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Welcome differing viewpoints and accept useful feedback.
- Focus criticism on ideas and work, not people.
- Take responsibility, apologize, and learn when harm occurs.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, and publishing another person's private information are
unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these
standards. Report conduct concerns privately through an available maintainer
contact or GitHub reporting mechanism. Maintainers will respect the reporter's
privacy and may remove, edit, or reject contributions or temporarily or
permanently exclude participants whose behavior is inappropriate.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html).
